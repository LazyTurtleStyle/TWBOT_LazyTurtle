Walks a barbarian village's loyalty ("toestemming") down with noble attacks until it is yours. Loyalty is tracked from your own reports (no report yet = assumed 100, plus regeneration over time); the overshoot guard never has more than ceil(loyalty / 35) nobles under way at once, so a lucky train can't drop a spare noble on a village you already own. With fewer nobles home it sends what's there and repeats after the round trip. Multiple nobles go out as separate attacks seconds apart. Auto-stops (never auto-resumes): conquered 🎉, taken by another player, red report, noble died without its hit landing, or the rally point refuses twice. Jobs start disarmed, arm them yourself.
