The in-game (premium) Account Manager builds, recruits and researches on its own, but it does not stay set up: its build queue tops out at 50 orders and falls idle when that runs dry. This page keeps a group → sjabloon plan and re-applies it every morning. Make and edit the sjablonen in TribalWars itself ("Sjablonen beheren"); only the assignment lives here. Storage (opslag) and delivery (leveringen) are not covered.
