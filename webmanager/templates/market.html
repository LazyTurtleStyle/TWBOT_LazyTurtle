The premium exchange is one market for the whole world. You can only sell into it while its stock is under the capacity, and the price is best right after someone has bought it out — windows that close within minutes. The watcher reads the exchange every few minutes (one page, whatever the number of villages, only in active hours) so you can see when those windows open on this world and what a good price is. Koers is the game's number: resources per premium point. Lower is better for selling.
