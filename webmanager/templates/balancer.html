Your big villages push resources to your small ones. This is the in-game send resources screen between your own villages — no trade ratio, no counter-party, just merchants moving goods. How much is sent is decided by how full the receiver is, never by what the sender can spare, so a rim village with a tiny warehouse never overflows.

The hard part is that a delivery already on the road is invisible to the receiver's own snapshot, so several senders in a row would each fill the same empty warehouse again. Every send is therefore written down together with the moment it lands, and anything still flying counts as already delivered. That bookkeeping is what this page shows.
