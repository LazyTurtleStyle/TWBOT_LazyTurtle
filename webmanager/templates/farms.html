The bot picks an attack per target by report freshness: A = re-scout (no/old report), B = minimal in-game template (stale report), C = exact loot attack from a fresh report. A & B run from in-game Farm Assistant templates you create in TribalWars; paste their ids below. Changes take effect on the next bot cycle.
