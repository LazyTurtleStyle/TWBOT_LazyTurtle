alpha A flag is not a per-village setting, which is the thing about flags that catches people out. They are an account-wide inventory: you own a number of them of each type and level, and every one you own sits on exactly one village. Giving village B a flag takes it off village A. You cannot have twenty villages on a resource flag while owning three of them.

So the plan below is group → flag type, applied for the whole account in one pass. Rows apply top to bottom and the later row wins: give every village the resource flag, then give [OFF] the attack flag. A village no row matches keeps its own flag_type. Where there are not enough flags to go round, the more specific row is served first and the rest are listed as unmet — nothing is ever taken off a village that already has what it wants.
